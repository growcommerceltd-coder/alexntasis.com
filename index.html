ClearSmile · Creator ad “Does it really work?” Watch the ad ↗
ClearSmile · Teeth whitening · Greece
“Does it really work?”
Angle
A skeptic's test, instead of the 50% offer every other ad led with.
What I did
Audited the account, then put the best-holding visual over the best-selling creator.
Result
4.86× ROAS and 459 shares. Account ROAS went from 2.68 to 4.84.

The process behind it

  1. 1. AuditTwo ads, strong in opposite placesThe UGC video turned 4.84% of clicks into buyers but only 23% kept watching. The reveal held 54% of viewers and sold half as well.
  2. 2. DecisionCombine themUse the reveal to earn the first seconds and a person to make the sale.
  3. 3. BriefReveal over the creator's questionThe purple gel reveal plays while she asks “Does it really work?”, then her story continues. No offer line.
  4. 4. Result4.86× ROAS on $3,003Hold rate stayed at 52%, 459 shares, and cost per purchase fell from $11.53 to $6.76.
Method

Winning ads aren't luck. They're a system.

Every brief is a hypothesis about the customer. The account tells me which ones were right.

  1. 01Customer researchReviews, ad comments, support tickets and forums, read for the customer's own words: what they want, what they fear, and what they tried before.
  2. 02Competitor and category researchWhat the category runs in the Ad Library and on TikTok. Ads that stay live for months are working; angles nobody is using are the opening.
  3. 03Account auditPerformance read by angle, hook and format, not ad by ad. Hook rate, hold rate, click-through and conversion show where each creative wins and where it leaks.
  4. 04Angle mapPersonas against angles against awareness stage. It shows what's been tested, what's missing, and what to make next, in priority order.
  5. 05Briefs built to be testedOne hypothesis per brief. Three to five hooks on the same body, so a result says which idea won, not which edit.
  6. 06Test, learn, iterateBudgets and kill rules set before launch. Winners get new hooks, formats and creators; every learning goes into a library the next brief starts from.
06 → 01Then it starts again. Every round of tests makes the next brief sharper.
